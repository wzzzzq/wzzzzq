## About Me
I am a junior undergraduate pursuing a B.S.E. in Computer Science at the **University of Michigan, Ann Arbor** and a B.E. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at **Shanghai Jiao Tong University**. I am currently a research intern in the [ARM Lab](https://arm.eecs.umich.edu/) at Michigan, advised by [Prof. Dmitry Berenson](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~dmitryb/).

**Research interest:** real-world reinforcement learning for robot foundation models — post-training generalist (VLA) policies directly on physical robots to make them reliable at precise, contact-rich, and long-horizon manipulation. 🤖
